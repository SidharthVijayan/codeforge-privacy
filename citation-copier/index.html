Privacy Policy for Citation Copier

Effective date: November 2, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 2, 2026

Summary

Citation Copier builds a citation for the web page you are viewing entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address and the details the page publishes about itself for citing: its meta tags (for example citation, Dublin Core and Open Graph tags), its structured data (JSON-LD), its canonical link, its title and its first heading. It does not read the article text, form fields, what you have typed, or other tabs. This "website content" is processed in memory in the extension's popup to show the citation and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab, once per click, to read the citation details listed above. It does not change the page.

When you press "Copy citation", the citation is placed on your clipboard using that click; the extension does not read your clipboard.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code. It does not look up DOIs or any other details online.

Data retention

Nothing is stored, including your chosen style, the access-date option and any edits you make to a citation. They last only while the popup is open. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
