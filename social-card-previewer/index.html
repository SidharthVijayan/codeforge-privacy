Privacy Policy for Social Card Previewer

Effective date: October 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary

Social Card Previewer is a Chrome extension that previews how the web page you are viewing may look when shared, and checks its Open Graph and X (Twitter) card tags. It works entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content to the developer or anyone else.

What the extension reads, and when

Only when you click the extension's toolbar button and then "Preview this page", it reads these parts of the current tab: its og: and twitter: meta tags, the page <title>, the meta description, the canonical link, and the page address. This "website content" is analyzed in memory inside the extension popup and discarded when the popup closes. The extension never runs in the background and never reads other tabs.

The preview image

To draw the preview and measure the image size, the popup loads the share image named in the page's own tags, directly from that image's address, just as your browser does when it displays the page. That request goes only to the server hosting the image (usually the website you are viewing), and it is sent without a referrer. The extension makes no other network requests and sends nothing to the developer.

Permissions and why they are needed

The extension requests only these two permissions:

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are viewing, and only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to any site in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab to collect the tags listed above. The script does not change the page.

It requests no host permissions, and no history, cookies, storage, downloads, or tabs permissions.

Data collection and sharing

  • No personal or sensitive user data is collected.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or remotely hosted code.
  • No data is sold, used for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Data retention

Nothing is stored. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage. The optional "Copy report" button places a plain-text report on your clipboard only when you click it; what you do with it afterwards is up to you.

Children

The extension is a developer and marketing tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
