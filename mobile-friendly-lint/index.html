Privacy Policy for Mobile-Friendly Lint

Effective date: November 9, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 9, 2026

Summary

Mobile-Friendly Lint checks the web page you are viewing for a missing or badly set viewport tag, content wider than the screen, and tap targets smaller than 48 px, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content beyond what is shown in the popup for that click.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address, protocol and title; the text of the page's viewport meta tag; the width of the viewport and of the page; and, for links, buttons, form controls and elements that run past the right edge, their on-screen size, tag name, id/class and a short visible label. It does not read anything you have typed into forms, cookies, or other tabs. This "website content" is processed in memory in the extension's popup and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab, once per click, to read the information listed above. It does not change the page.

When you press "Copy", the report is placed on your clipboard using that click; the extension does not read your clipboard. When you press "Download file", the file is created in your browser and saved through Chrome's normal download, without the downloads permission.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension, including your chosen export format; it lasts only while the popup is open. The only copy of the data is any file you choose to download or text you choose to copy. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
