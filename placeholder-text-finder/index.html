Privacy Policy for Placeholder Text Finder

Effective date: November 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 26, 2026

Summary

Placeholder Text Finder checks the page you are viewing for leftover placeholder content entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address, its title, meta description and social (Open Graph) title and description, the page's text (including hidden text), image alt text, tooltips and ARIA labels, and the addresses of its links and images. It skips scripts, styles, code samples and form fields, and it does not read what you have typed into forms, cookies, or other tabs. This "website content" is processed in memory to look for placeholder text and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs one small read-only script in that tab, once per click, that returns the page content listed above to the popup. If you press "Show" next to a finding, a second small script scrolls the page to that element and draws an outline box around it, which is removed after 2.5 seconds. The page's own content is not changed.

"Copy report" puts the report on your clipboard only when you press that button, and "Download CSV" saves a file to your computer through your browser's normal download, only when you press that button.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code. Links and images on the page are judged by their addresses only; the extension does not load them.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
