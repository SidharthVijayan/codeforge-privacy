Privacy Policy for Toolbar Pomodoro

Effective date: November 13, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary

Toolbar Pomodoro is a focus timer that runs entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, sell, or transmit any personal data. It never reads the web pages you visit, your tabs, or your browsing history.

What the extension stores

The extension saves only its own information, locally on your device in Chrome's extension storage: your timer settings (session lengths and the long break, auto-start and notification switches) and the timer's state (the current phase, when it ends or how much time is left, how many focus sessions are done in the current set, and the number of focus sessions and minutes completed today). This is not synced to other devices and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • alarms: schedules the end of the current focus or break session, and refreshes the minutes on the toolbar badge every 30 seconds while a session runs.
  • notifications: shows a notification when a session ends. You can turn this off in the extension's settings.
  • storage: keeps the settings and timer state described above on your device, so the timer continues when the popup is closed and after Chrome restarts.

The extension requests no access to websites and runs no scripts in web pages.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code.

Data retention

The settings and timer state stay on your device until you change them, press Reset (which clears the current timer but keeps today's count), or uninstall the extension. Today's count starts again from zero each day. The extension uses no cookies and no sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
