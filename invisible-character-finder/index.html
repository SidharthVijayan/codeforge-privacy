Privacy Policy for Invisible Character Finder

Effective date: November 23, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

Invisible Character Finder finds zero-width, hidden and look-alike characters in text, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address and title and one piece of text: the text of the text box, text area or editor you are typing in, or else the text you have selected on the page. It never reads password fields, other form fields, cookies, or other tabs. Text you paste into the popup's "Check other text" box stays in the popup. This "website content" is processed in memory in the extension's popup and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab, once per click, that returns the selected text or the text of the focused field. Only when you press "Clean this field" does it write the cleaned text back into that field (and "Undo" puts the original back); only when you press "Show on page" does it mark the affected words using the browser's built-in highlight feature, which does not change the page's content ("Clear highlights" or a reload removes the marks).

"Copy cleaned text" puts the cleaned text on your clipboard only when you press that button; the extension never reads your clipboard.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension; results last only while the popup is open. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
