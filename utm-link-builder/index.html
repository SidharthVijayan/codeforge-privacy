Privacy Policy for UTM Link Builder

Effective date: November 16, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

UTM Link Builder adds UTM tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content, utm_id) to the address of the page you are on, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content. The only things it keeps are your own presets, options and recently copied links, stored in your browser on this device.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the address (URL) of the tab you are on, so it can add your tags to it. It does not read the page's content, does not run any script in the page, and does not look at other tabs, cookies, or your browsing history.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. The extension uses it only to read that tab's address. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • storage: saves your presets (names and the tag values you typed), your options (lowercase, how spaces are handled, whether existing tags are replaced or kept), the name of the last preset you used, and the last 10 tagged links you copied. These are kept with chrome.storage.local, which stays on this device; the extension does not use Chrome sync storage, so they are not synced to your Google account by the extension.

When you press "Copy link", the link is placed on your clipboard using that click; the extension does not read your clipboard.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code. It does not open or check the links it builds.

Data retention and deletion

Presets and options stay until you delete them. Recent links are capped at the last 10; "Clear recent links" in the popup removes them, and "Delete" removes the selected preset. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored. Note that the recent links are full page addresses with your tags, so they can show which pages you tagged.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
