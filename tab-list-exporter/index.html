Privacy Policy for Tab List Exporter

Effective date: November 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 1, 2026

Summary

Tab List Exporter turns the titles and addresses of your open tabs into a list you can copy or save, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only while you have the extension's popup open, it reads the title and address (URL) of your open tabs: the tabs in the current window, the tabs you have selected, or the tabs in all windows, depending on the option you choose. It does not read the content of any web page, what you have typed, your history, bookmarks or closed tabs. Tabs from private (incognito) windows are never included in an export made from a normal window. This list is held in memory to show the preview and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • tabs: lets the extension read the title and address of your open tabs so it can list them for you. Chrome describes this permission as "Read your browsing history" because tab addresses are part of your browsing; the extension only reads the tabs that are open at the moment you open the popup, and only for that purpose.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics, and loads no remote code. When you click Copy, the list is placed on your clipboard; when you click Download, it is saved as a file on your computer. Where you paste or share it after that is up to you.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension, including your format and filter choices, which reset each time the popup closes. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.
