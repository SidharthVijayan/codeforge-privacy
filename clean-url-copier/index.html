Privacy Policy for Clean URL Copier

Effective date: November 19, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

Clean URL Copier removes tracking parameters (such as utm_source, fbclid and gclid) from the address of the page you are on and lets you copy the cleaned link, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the address (URL) and title of the tab you are on. The address is cleaned in the popup; the title is used only if you press "Copy as Markdown", to make a link like [Page title](link). It does not read the page's content, does not run any script in the page, and does not look at other tabs, cookies, or your browsing history.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. The extension uses it only to read that tab's address and title. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.

When you press "Copy clean link" or "Copy as Markdown", the text is placed on your clipboard using that click; the extension does not read your clipboard.

Data storage

The extension stores nothing. It has no storage permission; your choices in the popup (which parameters to keep or remove) are forgotten when the popup closes.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code. It does not open or check the links it cleans.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
