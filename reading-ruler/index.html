Privacy Policy for Reading Ruler

Effective date: November 24, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

Reading Ruler shows a tinted reading bar across the page you are reading and dims the rest of the page, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address and title and, for the page's paragraphs, list items, quotes and table cells: their font size, their line height and how many characters they hold, so the bar can fit whole lines of text. It does not read what the text says, what you have typed into forms, cookies, or other tabs. While the bar is on, it follows the up-and-down position of your mouse and your ↑, ↓ and Esc key presses in that tab, only to move or remove the bar; these are not recorded. This "website content" is processed in memory and is discarded when the popup closes or the bar is turned off.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab, once per click, to measure the page's text lines. When you press "Turn on ruler" (or change a setting while it is on), a second script adds the reading bar: one element with its own styles that lets clicks pass through and is hidden when printing. Esc, "Turn off ruler" or reloading the page removes it and leaves the page as it was.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension. The bar lasts until you turn it off or reload the page. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage, and your choices are not saved.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
