Privacy Policy for Keyboard Focus Order Visualizer

Effective date: November 21, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 21, 2026

Summary

Keyboard Focus Order Visualizer shows the order in which the Tab key moves through a web page, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address and title and, for the links, buttons, form fields and other elements that can take keyboard focus: their type, their tabindex, disabled and similar attributes, their position on the screen, and a short name (such as the link text or the field's label). It does not read what you have typed into forms, cookies, or other tabs. This "website content" is processed in memory in the extension's popup and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab, once per click, to read the page as described above, and a second script that draws numbered badges as a separate layer on top of the page. The layer ignores the mouse and is removed when you press Esc, click "Hide numbers", or reload the page. The page's own content is not changed.

When you press "Copy report", the text is placed on your clipboard using that click; the extension does not read your clipboard. When you press "Download CSV", your browser saves the file to your downloads folder like any other download.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension; results last only while the popup is open, and the numbers on the page last until you remove them or reload. The only copies of the data are any report you choose to copy or file you choose to download. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
