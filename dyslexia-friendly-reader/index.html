Privacy Policy for Dyslexia-Friendly Reader

Effective date: October 31, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 31, 2026

Summary

Dyslexia-Friendly Reader re-flows the article on the web page you are viewing into an easier-to-read layout entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address and title and either the text you have selected or the text of the page's main headings, paragraphs and lists. It does not read form fields, what you have typed, or other tabs. This "website content" is processed in memory to draw the reader view on that page and is discarded when you close the reader or leave the page.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab to read the selected text or the main article text, and then draws the reader view as an overlay on top of the page. Closing the reader (its Close button, the Esc key, or "Close reader" in the popup) removes the overlay. The page's own content is not changed.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics, loads no remote code and downloads no fonts; it uses fonts already installed on your computer.

Data retention

Nothing is stored, including your font, size, spacing and colour choices. They last only while the reader view is open on a page and reset to the defaults after you close it. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
