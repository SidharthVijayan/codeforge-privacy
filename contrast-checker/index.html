Privacy Policy for Contrast Checker

Effective date: October 28, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 28, 2026

Summary

Contrast Checker checks the colour contrast of the web page you are viewing entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address and, for each visible piece of text, the text itself, its computed text colour, background colours, opacity and font size. This "website content" is processed in memory to produce the on-screen report and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab to read the text colours and sizes. When you click "Show on page", it scrolls to that text and draws a temporary outline over it, which removes itself after a few seconds. It does not change the page's content.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics, and loads no remote code.

Data retention

Nothing is stored. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage. The "Copy report" button puts a text report on your clipboard only when you click it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
