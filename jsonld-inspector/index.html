Privacy Policy for JSON-LD Structured Data Inspector

Effective date: October 19, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 19, 2026

Summary

JSON-LD Structured Data Inspector is a Chrome extension that checks the structured data on the web page you are viewing. It works entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension reads, and when

Only when you click the extension's toolbar button, it reads the JSON-LD structured data blocks (<script type="application/ld+json">) on the current tab and counts any Microdata/RDFa items. This "website content" is analyzed in memory inside the extension popup and discarded when the popup closes. The extension never runs in the background and never reads other tabs.

Permissions and why they are needed

The extension requests only these two permissions:

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are viewing, and only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to any site in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab to collect its JSON-LD blocks. The script does not change the page.

It requests no host permissions, no history, cookies, storage, downloads, or tabs permissions.

Data collection and sharing

  • No personal or sensitive user data is collected.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or remotely hosted code.
  • No data is sold, used for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Data retention

Nothing is stored. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage. The optional "Copy report" button places a plain-text report on your clipboard only when you click it; what you do with it afterwards is up to you.

Children

The extension is a developer tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
