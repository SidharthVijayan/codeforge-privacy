Privacy Policy for Selection Word Counter

Effective date: November 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 14, 2026

Summary

Selection Word Counter counts the words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in text you choose, and estimates reading and speaking time. It works entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, sell, or transmit any personal data.

What the extension handles

The extension only sees text when you give it: when you select text on a page, right-click it and choose "Count words in selection", Chrome hands that selected text to the extension; or when you type or paste text into the extension's own box. It does not read the rest of the page, your tabs, or your browsing history, and it runs no scripts in web pages.

The last text you counted is kept in Chrome's session storage, which is held in memory only, so the popup can show it. It is cleared when you press Clear, when you count something new, or when you close the browser. Your reading and speaking speeds (two numbers) are saved in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • contextMenus: adds the "Count words in selection" item to the right-click menu when text is selected, and receives the selected text when you click it.
  • storage: keeps the last counted text in memory-only session storage for the popup, and saves your reading and speaking speeds locally.

The extension requests no access to websites (no host permissions) and has no content scripts.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code. "Copy counts" puts the numbers on your clipboard only when you click it.

Data retention

Counted text is never written to disk by the extension and disappears when the browser closes. The two speed settings stay on your device until you change them or uninstall the extension. The extension uses no cookies and no sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
