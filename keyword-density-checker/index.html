Privacy Policy for Keyword Density Checker

Effective date: November 25, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 25, 2026

Summary

Keyword Density Checker counts the words and phrases on the page you are viewing entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address, its title, its main (H1) headings and the visible text of the page's main area (headings, paragraphs, lists, quotes and table cells), or the text you have selected if you selected 20 or more words. It leaves out menus, sidebars, forms and hidden text, and it does not read what you have typed into forms, cookies, or other tabs. The focus keyword you type into the popup stays in the popup. This "website content" is processed in memory to count words and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs one small read-only script in that tab, once per click, that returns the page's text, title and H1 headings to the popup. It does not change the page.

"Copy table" puts the table on your clipboard only when you press that button, and "Download CSV" saves a file to your computer through your browser's normal download, only when you press that button.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage, and your choices are not saved.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
