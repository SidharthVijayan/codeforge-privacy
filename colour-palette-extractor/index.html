Privacy Policy for Colour Palette Extractor

Effective date: November 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 7, 2026

Summary

Colour Palette Extractor lists the dominant text, background and border colours used by visible elements in the web page you are viewing, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content beyond what is shown in the popup for that click.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address, protocol and title; the computed text colour, background colour (walking ancestors when transparent) and border colour of visible elements; and a short description of each element's tag. It does not read anything you have typed into forms, cookies, or other tabs. This "website content" is processed in memory in the extension's popup and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab, once per click, to read the colour information listed above. It does not change the page.

When you press "Copy", the report (or a single hex value) is placed on your clipboard using that click; the extension does not read your clipboard. When you press "Download file", the file is created in your browser and saved through Chrome's normal download, without the downloads permission.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension, including your chosen export format; it lasts only while the popup is open. The only copy of the data is any file you choose to download or text you choose to copy. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
