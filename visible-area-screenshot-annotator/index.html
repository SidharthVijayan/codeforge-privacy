Privacy Policy for Visible Area Screenshot Annotator

Effective date: November 20, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

Visible Area Screenshot Annotator takes a picture of the visible part of the tab you are on, when you click its button, and lets you draw on it and save or copy the result, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it captures an image of what is visible in the current tab and reads that tab's address (URL) and title. The image is shown in an editor tab opened by the extension. The address and title are used only to label the editor, to name the file you save (in the form screenshot-[site]-[date]-[time].png) and, only if you tick "Add page address and time", to print the address under the image. It does not run any script in the page, does not read the page's text or code, and does not look at other tabs, cookies, or your browsing history.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. The extension uses it only to capture the visible part of that tab and read its address and title. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.

"Save PNG" uses an ordinary browser download, and "Copy image" places the image on your clipboard using your click. The extension does not read your clipboard or your downloads.

Data storage

The extension stores nothing. It has no storage permission. The capture is passed from the extension's background worker to the editor tab in memory (and dropped from the worker as soon as the editor receives it, or after 60 seconds at most). Your drawings exist only in the editor tab and are discarded when you close or reload it. Files you choose to save go to your own downloads folder and are under your control.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code. Screenshots are never uploaded.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
