Privacy Policy for Hidden Text Detector

Effective date: November 17, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 17, 2026

Summary

Hidden Text Detector finds text on a web page that visitors can't see entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address and title, the text on the page, and the styles and positions that decide whether that text can be seen (for example display, visibility, opacity, font size, colours and where it sits on the page), plus the addresses of links inside hidden text. It does not read anything you have typed into forms, cookies, or other tabs. This "website content" is checked in memory in the extension's popup and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab, once per click, to read the page as described above. It does not change the page.

When you press "Copy report", the text is placed on your clipboard using that click; the extension does not read your clipboard. When you press "Download CSV", your browser saves the file to your downloads folder like any other download.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code. Link addresses found in hidden text are shown as text; the extension does not open or load them.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension; results last only while the popup is open. The only copies of the data are any report you choose to copy or file you choose to download. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
