Privacy Policy for Image Alt Text Auditor

Effective date: October 27, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 27, 2026

Summary

Image Alt Text Auditor is a Chrome extension that lists the images on the web page you are viewing and checks their alt text. It works entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content to the developer or anyone else.

What the extension reads, and when

Only when you click the extension's toolbar button and then "Audit images on this page", it reads these parts of the current tab: each image's address, alt, role, aria-hidden, aria-label, aria-labelledby and title attributes, its size on screen, whether it is inside a link or button (and that link's text), and the page address. This "website content" is analyzed in memory inside the extension popup and discarded when the popup closes. The extension never runs in the background and never reads other tabs.

"Show on page"

When you click "Show on page" next to an image, the extension scrolls the tab to that image and draws a temporary outline over it, which disappears after about two seconds. It does not change the page's content, and nothing is saved.

Permissions and why they are needed

The extension requests only these two permissions:

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are viewing, and only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to any site in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab to collect the image details listed above, and, only when you click "Show on page", a small script that scrolls to the image and draws the temporary outline.

It requests no host permissions, and no history, cookies, storage, downloads, or tabs permissions. It makes no network requests.

Data collection and sharing

  • No personal or sensitive user data is collected.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or remotely hosted code.
  • No data is sold, used for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Data retention

Nothing is stored. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage. The optional "Copy report" button places a plain-text report on your clipboard only when you click it; what you do with it afterwards is up to you.

Children

The extension is an accessibility and content-editing tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
