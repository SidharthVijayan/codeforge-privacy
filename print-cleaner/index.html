Privacy Policy for Print Cleaner

Effective date: November 22, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 22, 2026

Summary

Print Cleaner removes menus, ads, pop-ups and other clutter from a web page before you print it, entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension accesses

Only when you click the extension's button, it reads the current tab's address and title and, for the page's elements: their tag, id, class, role and label attributes, whether they are pinned to the screen, how much text they contain, and a short text snippet (used to show examples in the popup). It does not read what you have typed into forms, cookies, or other tabs. This "website content" is processed in memory in the extension's popup and is discarded when the popup closes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only after you click the extension. It gives no access to other tabs or to sites in the background.
  • scripting: runs a small read-only script in that tab, once per click, to find the article and the parts around it. When you press "Clean page" or "Print", a second script hides the parts you ticked by marking them with an attribute and adding a style (nothing is deleted), shows a small bar on the page (Print, Remove more, Undo, Restore page) and opens your browser's print dialog. If you choose to write link addresses on paper, it also marks each web link with its full address. "Restore page" or reloading the page removes all of these changes.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics and loads no remote code. Printing and "Save as PDF" are handled by your browser's own print dialog; the extension never sees the printed output.

Data retention

Nothing is stored by the extension; results last only while the popup is open, and the changes to the page last until you restore or reload it. The extension uses no cookies, local storage, or sync storage, and your choices are not saved.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
